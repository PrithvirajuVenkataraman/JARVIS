# FAQ Section in Settings

Simple answers to common questions, matching what users see under **Settings → About → FAQ**.

---

## Questions & Answers

### 1. How does Live Web Search find fresh information?
When Web Search is switched on, JARVIS checks live news, reference pages, and trusted public sources across the internet to bring back the newest facts instead of relying only on older background knowledge. Newer articles are prioritized so outdated pages do not show up as current news, and clickable source links are included so you can verify what was found.

### 2. What happens if free daily limits run out?
You can add your own personal free API keys anytime under **API Keys (BYOK)** to keep going without shared limits. Even when online image limits run out, JARVIS can still read text in screenshots and recognize everyday photos right on your device so you are never left completely stuck.

### 3. What does the Think button do?
Tapping the brain icon next to the message box turns on deeper thinking mode. Use it whenever you want JARVIS to slow down and work carefully through tricky math, coding, or multi-step questions. You can turn it back off anytime by tapping the **Think ×** tag above the input box.

### 4. Where are my chats, attachments, and saved memories kept?
All of your conversations, custom instructions, and saved memories stay privately on your own device and browser — we do not store your personal chat history on our servers. You can download the current chat as a Markdown file anytime using **Export Conversation**, or save a password-protected backup under **Backup & storage** to move your chats to another device.

### 5. How do Saved Memories work, and is my private info protected?
When Personalization is turned on, JARVIS picks up helpful preferences and details you share (like your name, city, or preferred reply style) and rewrites them clearly so they make sense later on. You can view, edit, export, or delete any saved memory anytime. Built-in safety checks also block accidental sharing of passwords, card numbers, and private keys before messages leave your device.

### 6. What kinds of photos, documents, and helpers are supported?
You can upload standard photos, iPhone photos, camera RAW pictures, PDFs, Word documents, spreadsheets, and text files (large photos and uncommon formats are automatically prepared on your device first). JARVIS can also check live web results, open web pages, look up the current date and time, and run quick calculations for you.
